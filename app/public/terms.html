1. What HexCall is

HexCall is a free, informational statistics site for Teamfight Tactics, operated by Khanh (buinguyenbaokhanh). It aggregates publicly available match results into composition, champion, item and trait statistics, and — from the item components you tell it you are holding — suggests what to build, which units have performed best holding it, and which compositions that leads toward.

2. It is information, not advice

Everything here is a description of what has happened in past matches. Averages drawn from past games do not predict any individual game, samples can be small or stale, and a statistic can be wrong or out of date after a patch. You use the site at your own risk and remain responsible for your own decisions in-game.

3. You enter your own board state

HexCall does not read your game, your client, or your screen, and does not receive any live game state. You type in the components you are holding. The site has no way to know what is happening in a match you are playing.
